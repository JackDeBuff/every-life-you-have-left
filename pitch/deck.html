Isn’t this just ChatGPT?ChatGPT tells one story in a chat bubble. This holds a life consistent to 70, cites your words and a base rate at every turn, and splits when you choose.
Isn’t it a horoscope?No. Every event is traced to what you said, the odds come from real data, and we never claim to predict.
What happens to my data?Incognito by default: nothing saved, erased after 24 hours. Saving is opt-in, for 30 days. We never ask your race. Adults only.
Could it hurt someone?No death or serious illness for you. Worrying text shows the 988 crisis line.
Does it change behavior?We have not tested that yet. MIT’s 2024 study (344 young adults) found an AI future self raised connection and lowered anxiety right after.

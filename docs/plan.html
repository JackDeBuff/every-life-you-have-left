How it works

One person, four lives, about three minutes

The judge's path takes about three minutes. The deep path has no limit: more questions, more pauses, uploads, and a digital twin to talk to at the end.

  1. ExplainerWhat you'll see and what happens to your data
  2. ConsentWe save your data to improve it. 18+.
  3. SetupQuick or Deep. Voice or text.
  4. UploadsCV, LinkedIn PDF, your AI's export. Optional.
  5. What I learnedEditable cards about you
  6. Questions5 for Quick. Adaptive and unlimited for Deep.
  7. Four lives playDrawn while they stream in
  8. PauseYour answer re-tunes all four lives
  9. At 70Four verdicts side by side
  10. PostcardsDownload, or share a link
  11. Talk to future youLetters, or your digital twin

Quick · the judge

5 open questions, 1 pause, letters read aloud in an older voice. About 2.5 to 3 minutes.

Deep · as long as you want

Uploads, adaptive questions aimed at what you worry about, 4–5 pauses, and a twin that writes the way you do.

The four lives · two axes: bold or safe, for others or for yourself

Your Path

If nothing changes. Your pause answers steer it.

"Steady, and quieter than you planned."

The Leap

Says yes to the big decision you keep coming back to.

"Louder than you planned."

The Careful Life

Fear wins. The most money, and the boldness and connection regrets.

"Safe, and you know exactly what it cost."

The Unjudged Life

What you said you'd do if nobody judged you.

"Poorer on paper, richer every Tuesday."

Pitch · full scripts and Q&A in research/05

How we hook the judge

The opening line · Supawich's hook"Every interview asks where you see yourself in five years. Nobody can answer it. Give me 90 seconds and you'll have four answers, and you'll know which one is yours."
30-second versionYour brain thinks about your future self the way it thinks about a stranger, and people don't plan for strangers. Every Life You Have Left plays out four lives you could still live, year by year, from what you tell it. Every turning point is traced to your own words and a real-world statistic, so it's not a horoscope. At the end you walk away with a real answer to "where do you see yourself in five years". Students use it free; career centers license it as interview prep. Give me 90 seconds and I'll show you yours.
"Isn't this just ChatGPT?"ChatGPT tells one flattering story. We hold four lives consistent for 45 years, and each turn links to what you said. Saturday's ChatGPT control group turns this answer into data.
"Isn't this a horoscope?"Every turn shows your sentence and a cited base rate. We claim vividness, not prediction.
"Who pays?"Free for students, licensed by career centers at about $3 per student a year, then the next crossroads.
"You save my data?"Yes, and the consent screen says so in one plain sentence. It's used to make the lives more realistic.
